How ranks are calculated & sources

We estimate one-rep max with Epley: weight × (1 + reps / 30); a single rep uses its actual weight. For pull-ups and dips, weight includes your body weight. Sets of 1–12 reps qualify. This is an estimate, not a requirement to test a maximum.

Strength Level’s beginner, novice, intermediate, advanced and elite benchmarks anchor Bronze 1, Silver 1, Platinum 1, Diamond 1 and Master 3. Other ranks interpolate linearly. Chang 3 is 115% of elite. All 24 ranks are Time to Chang’s own progression, not published percentiles.

Pull-up and dip ratios use published weighted standards at 75 kg male / 60 kg female, scaled to your body weight. Other lifts use published bodyweight ratios. Machine setups differ; compare on the same equipment. Reference data accessed 24 September 2026.
